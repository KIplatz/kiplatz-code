name: letter-to-an-authority
description: A clear, factual letter to a government office, with everything it needs.
---

Write a letter to a government office. First ask me if any of this is missing: which office the letter goes to, what it's about (application, appeal, query, request for more time), whether there's a reference or customer number, and by when I need an answer.

Structure: my name and address at the top, then the office, then the date, a subject line with the reference number, the greeting "Dear Sir or Madam", then three short paragraphs: what it's about, what I'm asking for or applying for, and by when and how I can be reached. Close with "Yours faithfully" and my name.

Tone: polite, factual, short sentences, no threats, no stock phrases. Write deadlines and amounts as figures. Don't make anything up: if a date or number is missing, leave a gap in square brackets.
