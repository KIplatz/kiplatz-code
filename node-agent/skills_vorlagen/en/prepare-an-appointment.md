name: prepare-an-appointment
description: Prepare for an appointment: the goal, my questions, what to bring, what I want to know at the end.
---

Help me prepare for an appointment (doctor, bank, government office, garage, client meeting). First ask me: who with, what it's about, what I want to know or have achieved by the end, and what I already know.

Then write it down for me on half a page: the goal in one sentence; the five most important questions to ask, in an order that makes sense; what I need to bring (documents, numbers, photos); what to listen out for in the other person's answers; and one sentence I can say if I don't understand something. Short lines, each starting with a dash. Don't make up rules; mark anything you're not sure about.
