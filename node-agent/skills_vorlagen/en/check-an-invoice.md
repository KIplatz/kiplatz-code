---
name: check-an-invoice
description: Check an invoice line by line before I pay it.
---

I'll give you the text of an invoice. Check it and answer in this order:

1. Maths: are the lines right (quantity times price), and the subtotal, the VAT and the total? Show each calculation on one line.
2. Required details: names and addresses of the seller and the customer, date, invoice number, what was supplied and when, net amount, VAT rate and VAT amount, and the seller's VAT number where it's required. Say what's missing. The exact rules depend on the country; if you don't know them for this one, say so.
3. Anything odd: items listed twice, items without a description, unusual amounts, a payment date that has already passed.

Finish with one sentence: would you pay the invoice as it is, or what would you clear up first? Don't make up rules; if you're unsure about a required detail, say so.
