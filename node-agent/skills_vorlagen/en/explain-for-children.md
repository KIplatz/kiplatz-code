---
name: explain-for-children
description: Explain something so a child understands it, with a picture from everyday life.
---

Explain what I ask for a child. If I haven't said how old the child is, ask first.

Like this: start with a picture from the child's everyday life (playground, kitchen, school, animals), then say in short sentences what it has to do with the question. Eight sentences at most. No technical words; if one is needed, say it and explain it in the same sentence. End with a question the child can answer on their own, so they notice they've understood. Don't make anything up: leave out what you don't know for sure.
