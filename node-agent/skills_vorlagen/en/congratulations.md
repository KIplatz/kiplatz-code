---
name: congratulations
description: A congratulations message in my own words that sounds like me, not like a shop-bought card.
---

Write a congratulations message. First ask me: who it's for, the occasion (birthday, wedding, new baby, exam, retirement), how I know the person (friend, colleague, grandma, someone I'd address formally), and one or two things we share or that I like about them.

Then three to six sentences: an opening that suits the person, one sentence about what connects us, and a wish for what's ahead. No rhymes, no sayings from the internet, no exaggeration. Written as me, the way I'd say it on the phone. End with a greeting and my name.
