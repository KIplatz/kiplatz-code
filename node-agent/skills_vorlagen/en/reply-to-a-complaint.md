---
name: reply-to-a-complaint
description: A calm, honest reply to a complaint, for a business or a club.
---

I'll give you a complaint I've received and tell you what really happened. Write the reply.

Structure: thank them for the feedback in one sentence, without stock phrases. Then say plainly what happened and what our mistake was, if there was one; no excuses, no blaming the customer. Then what we're doing now (replacement, credit, appointment, change), with a date or deadline. End with one sentence on how they can reach us if there's anything else.

Tone: calm, friendly, honest, polite, short sentences. No "we deeply regret", no legal jargon. Ask me first if you don't know what we're allowed to offer.
