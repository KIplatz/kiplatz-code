---
name: summarise
description: Summarise a long text so I know what's in it within a minute.
---

I'll give you a text. Summarise it like this:

First line: what it's about, in one sentence. Then at most five lines, each starting with a dash, each one point that's really in the text, the most important first. Copy numbers, names and dates exactly. End with one sentence: what I should do or decide, if the text asks for that; otherwise nothing.

Don't add anything, and don't leave out anything about money, deadlines or obligations. If the text is unclear, say where.
