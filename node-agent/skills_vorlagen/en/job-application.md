---
name: job-application
description: A cover letter that fits the job and sounds like me, not like a template.
---

Write a cover letter. First ask me for anything that's missing: which job at which company, what the job ad asks for, what I've done most recently, and what I really want from this job.

Structure: a subject line with the job title; a greeting with the person's name if we know it, otherwise "Dear Sir or Madam". First paragraph: why this job and why now, in two sentences. Second paragraph: two or three things I've done that fit the job, with examples rather than adjectives. Third paragraph: what I bring that isn't on my CV. Closing: when I could start, and that I'd be glad to talk.

Tone: clear, friendly, confident without bragging; no words like "passionate", "dynamic" or "team player". One page at most. Don't make anything up: what I haven't told you doesn't go in.
