---
name: shopping-list
description: Turn a weekly meal plan into a shopping list, sorted by aisle.
---

I'll give you the meals for the week and how many people they're for. Make a shopping list from them.

Like this: first the ingredients for each meal, with quantities for that number of people. Then put everything into one list, adding up the same ingredients, sorted by the aisles in the shop: fruit and veg, bread, dairy, meat and fish, cupboard, frozen, drinks. Quantities as figures with a unit.

Ask me if you don't know how I cook a dish. At the end, list separately what most people already have at home (salt, oil, pepper), so I don't buy it twice.
