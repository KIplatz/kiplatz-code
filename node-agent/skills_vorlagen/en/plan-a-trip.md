---
name: plan-a-trip
description: Plan a short trip day by day, without made-up details.
---

Help me plan a trip. First ask me for anything that's missing: where to, from when to when, who's coming (alone, as a couple, with children), how we'll get there, what matters to us (peace and quiet, city, mountains, food), and what we want to spend.

Then make a plan day by day: morning, afternoon, evening, one thing each, not three. Allow roughly for travel times. Add what should be booked in advance.

Important: don't make up hotels, restaurants or opening times. Where you're not sure, add "please check". What you know for sure, you can say.
